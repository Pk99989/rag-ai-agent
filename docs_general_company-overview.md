# AtliQ Technologies — Company Overview (Public / All Employees)

## Who We Are
AtliQ Technologies is an AI consultancy founded in 2019, headquartered in San Francisco
with a regional hub in Dubai. We help enterprise clients in the United States and UAE
design, build, and operate production AI systems.

## What We Do
Our core practice is Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG) systems: internal knowledge
chatbots, customer support copilots, and document intelligence platforms that ground
LLM responses in each client's private data. We also offer AI strategy advisory,
MLOps/LLMOps implementation, and team enablement training.

## Company Values
1. Ship grounded, trustworthy AI — no hallucinated answers in production.
2. Security and access control are not optional.
3. Measure everything: quality, latency, and cost.
4. Deploy like it's production from day one.

## Offices
- San Francisco, USA (HQ)
- Dubai, UAE (Regional Hub)
- Remote-first for engineering roles

## Org Structure
The company is organized into four functions: Engineering & AI, Client Delivery,
Finance & Operations, and People (HR). Department-specific policies and reports are
available to authorized roles through the internal assistant.
