# AtliQ Technologies — FY2025 Financial Report (CONFIDENTIAL — Finance & Executive Only)

## Summary
AtliQ Technologies closed fiscal year 2025 with total revenue of $48.2M, up 21% year-over-year
from $39.8M in FY2024. Net profit margin improved to 14.6%, compared to 11.2% the prior year.

## Revenue by Region
- United States: $27.1M (56%)
- United Arab Emirates: $12.9M (27%)
- Rest of World: $8.2M (17%)

## Revenue by Business Line
- AI Consulting Services: $31.4M
- Managed RAG Platform Subscriptions: $11.8M
- Training & Enablement: $5.0M

## Operating Expenses
- Salaries & Benefits: $18.6M
- Cloud Infrastructure (Azure/AWS/GCP): $4.1M
- Sales & Marketing: $6.3M
- G&A: $3.2M

## Outlook
FY2026 revenue guidance is $58M–$62M, driven by expansion of the Managed RAG Platform
and new enterprise contracts in the UAE financial services sector. This document contains
non-public financial information and must not be shared outside the Finance and Executive
teams.
