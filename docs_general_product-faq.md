# Internal Assistant — Product FAQ (Public / All Employees)

**Q: What can I ask this assistant?**
A: Anything covered in company documents you're authorized to access — company overview,
and (if your role permits) HR policy or finance reports.

**Q: Why did it say it doesn't have information about something?**
A: The assistant only answers from retrieved company documents. If nothing relevant is
found, or your role doesn't have access to the relevant department's documents, it will
say so rather than guessing.

**Q: Is my chat history stored?**
A: Queries, response metadata, latency, and token cost are logged for monitoring and
evaluation purposes. Raw chat content is not shared outside the AI Platform team.

**Q: Who do I contact if the assistant gives a wrong or concerning answer?**
A: Report it to the AI Platform team at ai-platform@atliq-tech.example so it can be
added to the evaluation regression set.
