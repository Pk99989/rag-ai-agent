# AtliQ Technologies — Employee Handbook (CONFIDENTIAL — HR & Executive Only)

## Leave Policy
Full-time employees accrue 24 days of paid annual leave per year, plus 10 public holidays.
Sick leave is capped at 12 days per year and does not carry over. Parental leave is 16 weeks
for primary caregivers and 4 weeks for secondary caregivers, fully paid.

## Working Hours
Standard working hours are Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM–6:00 PM local time, with a flexible
2-hour window for start times. Remote work is permitted up to 3 days per week for
roles not requiring on-site client presence.

## Code of Conduct
Employees must not disclose confidential client or company data outside authorized
channels. Any suspected data leak must be reported to hr-compliance@atliq-tech.example
within 24 hours.

## Performance Reviews
Reviews are conducted twice yearly (June and December). Ratings feed into the annual
compensation review cycle managed jointly by HR and department leads.
