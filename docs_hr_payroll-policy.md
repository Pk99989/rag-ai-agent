# Payroll Policy & Processing Schedule (CONFIDENTIAL — HR & Executive Only)

## Pay Cycle
AtliQ Technologies runs payroll on a monthly cycle. Salaries are disbursed on the last
working day of each month via direct bank transfer.

## Payroll Processing Timeline
- Day 1–20: Timesheet and expense submission window
- Day 21–25: Manager approval and payroll reconciliation
- Day 26–28: Finance sign-off
- Last working day: Salary disbursement

## Deductions
Standard deductions include applicable income tax withholding, statutory retirement
contributions (401k in the US, GPSSA in the UAE), and health insurance premiums where
opted in.

## Bonus & Commission Payouts
Annual performance bonuses are paid in January, tied to the December review cycle.
Sales commission is paid monthly, one cycle in arrears.

For payroll discrepancies, contact payroll-team@atliq-tech.example. This document
